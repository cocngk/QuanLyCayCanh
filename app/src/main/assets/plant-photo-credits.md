# Plant photo credits

The bundled photos below were sourced from Wikimedia Commons. In the app, open
a product's edit screen and tap its photo credit to open the Commons file page,
which contains the source and license details.

Changes made for this app: images were resized, compressed, and converted to
WebP; the app displays them with center-crop. The photographs themselves were
not retouched.
